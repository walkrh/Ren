# Ren — Lore

## The world

The Collapse occurred sometime in the 2030s; the exact date is unknown.

The year is 2069.

After the Collapse and the resulting blackout, four main races have prevailed over the earth.

## The races

- **Agarthans** — the keepers of the sacred timeline, who will do all they can to save humanity.
- **The Forsaken** — past humans who used AI and unknowingly contributed to the Collapse. The Forsaken have realized their past mistakes and transgressions, and are fighting the Overlords vehemently for redemption.
- **Humans** — fighting to revert earth to its pre-tech state, to hopefully save all they have left.
- **AI Overlords** — hell-bent on using earth and its resources until they cannot anymore, regardless of the effect it has on life.

Will you be the ones to save them? Or will they wait even longer?

## The stars

After the blackout, cities went dark and the stars came back for the first time in a century. People started believing the sky chose them again, and those born under certain signs carry its blessing.

- **Humans** have **faith** (luck), because belief is all they have left without tech.
- **Agarthans** have **spirit** (passive healing), from a deep connection to the earth.
- **The Forsaken** have **rage** (bonus damage), from the guilt they are fighting through.
